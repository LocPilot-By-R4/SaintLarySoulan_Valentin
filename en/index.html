Things to do around Saint-Lary-Soulan

In the heart of the Aure Valley, Le Cocon de Saint-Lary is a convenient base for enjoying the French Pyrenees in every season. Skiing, high-altitude lakes, hiking, mountain biking, white-water activities, wellness and village life are all part of the experience around Saint-Lary-Soulan.

Winter

Saint-Lary skiing & Pla d’Adet

Reach the mountain sectors from the village and enjoy the Saint-Lary ski area for skiing and snowboarding.

Discover Saint-Lary in winter →
Nature

Néouvielle Nature Reserve

Explore the lakes of Orédon, Aubert, Aumar, Oule or Bastan and discover one of the landmark natural areas of the Hautes-Pyrénées.

Plan a Néouvielle outing →
Outdoor

MTB, canyoning, rafting & paragliding

On foot, by bike, on the water or in the air, the Aure Valley offers a wide range of mountain activities throughout your stay.

See outdoor activities →
Wellness

Sensoria Rio & Balnéa

Enjoy a wellness break at Sensoria Rio in Saint-Lary-Soulan, or discover Balnéa in Loudenvielle depending on opening conditions.

Hiking in the Aure Valley
